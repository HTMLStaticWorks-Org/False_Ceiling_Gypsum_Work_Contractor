Structural Anatomy

Inside the Vertex 5-Layer Engineering Standard

What happens behind the smooth painted plaster determines whether a ceiling sags, cracks, or lasts 25+ years without deflection.

Layer 01 — Structural Anchor Soffit Rawl Plugs & Soffit Cleats

High-tensile zinc-plated metallic rawl expansion bolts anchored directly into solid RCC concrete slab with 40kg pull-out load testing.

Layer 02 — Framing Grid 0.50mm Galvanized Steel Channels

Heavy-gauge GI ceiling sections spaced tightly at 450mm c/c with perimeter wall angles and intermediate cross-connectors resisting thermal contraction.

Layer 03 — Acoustic Buffer Rockwool Thermal-Acoustic Core

Optional 50mm non-flammable mineral fiber matting providing ambient room sound dampening and superior thermal roof insulation.

Layer 04 — Sheathing Substrate Saint-Gobain Gyproc 12.5mm Boards

Tapered-edge certified plasterboards scored and fastened with rust-proof cadmium-plated drywall bugle screws every 200mm.

Layer 05 — Finishing Layer Paper Joint Tape & Level-5 Skim

Cross-fiber paper joint reinforcement tape embedded in polymer jointing compound, finished with two fine coats of mirror-smooth silk putty.

Quality Inspection

Why Cheap Ceilings Crack

  • Flimsy 0.30mm Steel: Local contractors cut costs with paper-thin metal that warps under ceiling fan vibration.
  • Mesh vs Paper Tape: Plastic mesh tape snaps during seasonal humidity swings; our cross-fiber paper tape never tears.
  • Missing Perimeter Support: Skipping the wall perimeter angle creates corner hairline cracks within 6 months.
  • Random Fastener Spacing: Screws placed over 300mm apart cause board bellies and uneven joint shadows.
