Material Selection

Gypsum Board vs Traditional POP

Choosing the right material substrate determines ceiling lifespan, surface precision, and installation speed.

Evaluation Factor Saint-Gobain Gypsum Board Handcrafted POP Plaster Vertex Recommendation
Surface Flatness Factory-manufactured precision (+/- 0.5mm) Depends on mason hand skill Gypsum for modern linear rooms
Turnaround Time Fast dry construction (3–7 days) Requires drying & curing (10–18 days) Gypsum saves 60% site timeline
Weight & Sag Resistance Lightweight on galvanized GI channels Heavier mass on wire mesh Gypsum guarantees zero sag
Curve & Moulding Detailing Best for step trays, geometric boxes Exceptional for intricate hand carvings POP for classical domes & florals
