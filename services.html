Service Discipline 01

Gypsum Board False Ceilings

Drywall false ceiling systems installed using genuine Saint-Gobain Gyproc 12.5mm boards and heavy-gauge galvanized framing. Ideal for living rooms, master bedrooms, and commercial offices.

Key Engineering Specifications:

  • Framing Gauge: 0.50mm BMT zinc-coated galvanized steel channels
  • Board Options: Standard Regular, Moisture-Resistant (MR), and Fire-Rated (FR)
  • Minimum Drop: 75mm (Plain Level) to 125mm (Stepped Cove)
  • Fasteners: Corrosion-resistant cadmium drywall screws every 200mm
Turnaround: 5–8 days 15-Year Sag Warranty Level-5 Silk Finish

Master craftsmen sculpting freeform curves, diamond coffers, intricate cornices, and chandelier medallions using fine Plaster of Paris (POP). Ideal for dining pavilions, villas, and grand entrance foyers.

Architectural Cove & Magnetic Track Lighting

A ceiling design is only as good as its light wash. We integrate concealed cove LED channels, magnetic low-voltage track rails, wall grazing diffusers, and smart DALI dimming from the design stage.

Lighting Integration Standard:

  • Glare-Free Coves: Radiused upstand prevents hot spotting and dot reflection
  • Flush Magnetic Tracks: 48V safe low-voltage rails with modular spot and line heads
  • Tunable CCT Kelvin: Warm dim from 2400K (evening relax) to 4000K (productive day)
  • Serviceable Transformers: Concealed in ventilated magnetic access hatches
24V High-CRI 90+ Strips Zero Dotting Diffusers Smart Dimmer Ready

Execution Methodology

Our 6-Phase Installation Roadmap

Every project is managed with rigorous stage-gate inspections from survey to final snag handover.

01

Laser Survey & 3D Plan

Digital laser scans calibrate RCC slab flatness, identify structural beams, and map conduits into a millimetric 3D CAD layout.

02

Structural GI Grid Framing

0.50mm BMT zinc-coated channels bolted with heavy rawl expansion anchors at 450mm centers, verified with precision spirit level.

03

Service Integration & Infill

Concealed electrical lines, HVAC diffusers, and 50mm acoustic rockwool sound damping installed before ceiling closure.

04

Precision Gyproc Boarding

Saint-Gobain 12.5mm boards mounted with staggered transverse joints and cadmium anti-corrosion drywall screws every 200mm.

05

Tape Jointing & Level-5 Silk Skim

Micro-perforated joint tape bedded in polymer compound, followed by 2 skim coats and dust-free orbital machine vacuum sanding.

06

Lighting Test & Snag Handover

Full 24-hour cove LED continuous burn-in, lux level verification, downlight checks, and signed 15-year warranty handover.
